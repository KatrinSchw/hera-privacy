Hera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version: everything you put into Hera stays on your phone. There's no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. We can't see your data, and we don't sell or share it.

Who we are

Hera is made by Katrin Schwab, Heilbronn, Germany. For anything about privacy, write to hera.femaleperformance@gmail.com.

What Hera stores, and where

Hera saves what you enter in the app's own storage on your device. That includes:

  • your answers from setup, like your cycle length and the start of your last period
  • period days and flow
  • your daily check-ins, like energy, mood and symptoms
  • notes and journal entries
  • tasks, routines, goals and reminders
  • app settings, like appearance and language

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Hera works without an internet connection.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Hera asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself and their content doesn't leave it. You can switch notifications off any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Backups

Because Hera's data lives on your phone, it's included in your iPhone's normal backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you use them. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Beta testing with TestFlight

While Hera is in beta, it's installed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple shares some information with us so we can run the beta: your name and email if we invited you by email, which build you installed and when, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This information never includes what you logged in Hera. Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy. We use it only to fix and improve Hera, and you can ask us to delete it.

Deleting your data

Delete the Hera app and everything it stored on your phone is deleted with it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. For Hera that's the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg. Since your Hera data stays on your device, you control it directly. For anything else, like your TestFlight details, email us and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If Hera ever starts sending data off your phone, for example to sync between devices, we'll update this policy first and ask you before turning that on.
