Hera

Cycle Syncing Planner for iPhone

Most planners treat every day the same. Your energy doesn't.

Hera is a planner for your tasks, goals and routines that knows where you are in your cycle. Log your period and how you feel, and Hera shows what today is good for: when to take on the big things, and when to go easier on yourself.

What's inside

  • Today: your cycle day, expected energy and what fits today, right above your to-do list.
  • Tasks and routines: priorities, subtasks, alarms, and routines for every day or only the phases you choose.
  • Goals: things to do more of or less of, in small steps.
  • Cycle and Insights: log your period, see what comes next, and what your own check-ins show over time.
  • Optional: Apple Health for your period days, and Friends to share chosen tasks and goals with people you pick.

Private by design. Everything you log stays on your phone. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Only the optional Friends feature uses an account.

Hera is not a medical device and not for contraception.
